Formula library

Searchable engineering formula sheets

Organized by algebra, trigonometry, calculus, physics mechanics, statics, dynamics, mechanics of materials, thermodynamics, fluid mechanics, heat transfer, and machine design.

Algebra

Quadratic Formula

x = (-b +/- sqrt(b^2 - 4ac)) / 2a

a, b, c: polynomial coefficients

x: roots of the equation

Units: Coefficients must be in compatible units for the modeled quantity.

Use case: Solving a parabolic trajectory or design constraint.

Trigonometry

Sine and Cosine Components

Fx = F cos(theta), Fy = F sin(theta)

F: vector magnitude

theta: angle from positive x-axis

Units: Components have the same units as the original vector.

Use case: Resolving a cable force into horizontal and vertical components.

Calculus

Derivative Definition

f'(x) = lim(h -> 0) [f(x + h) - f(x)] / h

f(x): function

h: small change in input

Units: Derivative units are output units divided by input units.

Use case: Finding velocity from displacement or heat rate from energy.

Calculus

Definite Integral

Integral from a to b of f(x) dx

f(x): rate or density

a, b: interval limits

Units: Integral units combine the function units and input units.

Use case: Accumulating distributed load into total force.

Physics Mechanics

Newton's Second Law

Sum F = m a

Sum F: net force

m: mass

a: acceleration

Units: Use newtons, kilograms, and meters per second squared.

Use case: Predicting acceleration of a moving machine carriage.

Physics Mechanics

Work and Kinetic Energy

U = Delta T = 1/2 m v2^2 - 1/2 m v1^2

U: work

m: mass

v1, v2: speeds

Units: Work and kinetic energy are measured in joules.

Use case: Estimating required actuator work.

Statics

Moment of a Force

M = r x F

r: position vector

F: force vector

M: moment vector

Units: Moment is commonly reported in N m.

Use case: Calculating support reactions for a loaded beam.

Statics

Planar Equilibrium

Sum Fx = 0, Sum Fy = 0, Sum M = 0

Fx, Fy: force components

M: moments about a point

Units: Use consistent force and length units.

Use case: Solving reactions at a pin and roller support.

Dynamics

Impulse and Momentum

Integral F dt = m v2 - m v1

F: force

t: time

m: mass

v: velocity

Units: Impulse units are N s, equivalent to kg m/s.

Use case: Sizing a stopper for a moving cart.

Mechanics of Materials

Axial Stress

sigma = P / A

sigma: normal stress

P: axial load

A: cross-sectional area

Units: Stress is force per area, commonly Pa or MPa.

Use case: Checking if a tie rod is below allowable stress.

Mechanics of Materials

Beam Flexure Formula

sigma = M y / I

M: bending moment

y: distance from neutral axis

I: area moment of inertia

Units: Keep force, length, and area moment units consistent.

Use case: Estimating maximum stress in a loaded beam.

Thermodynamics

First Law of Thermodynamics

Delta U = Q - W

Delta U: change in internal energy

Q: heat added

W: work done by system

Units: Energy terms are measured in joules or kilojoules.

Use case: Analyzing piston-cylinder compression.

Fluid Mechanics

Continuity Equation

rho A V = constant

rho: density

A: flow area

V: average velocity

Units: For incompressible flow, A1 V1 = A2 V2.

Use case: Finding velocity change through a nozzle.

Fluid Mechanics

Bernoulli Equation

p/rho g + V^2/2g + z = constant

p: pressure

rho: density

V: velocity

z: elevation

Units: Each term has units of length when using SI head form.

Use case: Estimating pressure differences in a pipe or duct.

Heat Transfer

Conduction Heat Transfer

q = -k A dT/dx

q: heat transfer rate

k: conductivity

A: area

dT/dx: temperature gradient

Units: Heat transfer rate is watts.

Use case: Estimating heat loss through a wall.

Machine Design

Gear Speed Ratio

omega_out / omega_in = N_in / N_out

omega: angular speed

N: gear teeth count

Units: Speed ratio is dimensionless.

Use case: Selecting gears to reduce motor speed.
